Create an invoice

  1. Open Invoices → New invoice.
  2. Customer: start typing a name. If they already exist, MTL links to them and fills in their details; if not, the new customer is saved for next time.
  3. Line items: add a description, quantity, and rate for each thing you're billing. The amounts and total calculate as you type.
  4. Add tax, shipping, or a discount if needed, set the issue and due dates, and (for job costing) pick a Job.
Taxable vs. not: each line has a tax checkbox, so you can charge tax on some items and not others on the same invoice.
Two buttons

Save draft vs. Save & send

ButtonWhat happens
Save draftSaves the invoice so you can review or finish it later. Nothing is emailed.
Save & sendSaves it and emails it to the customer's address right away, with a payment link.
If an email can't send (for example, no customer email on file), MTL tells you and keeps the invoice as a draft so nothing is lost.
Getting paid

Record a payment

When a customer pays, record it against the invoice so your books and receivables stay accurate.

  1. Open the invoice and choose to record a payment.
  2. Enter the amount and the date it arrived (that date drives your cash-basis reports).
  3. MTL marks the invoice Partial or Paid and posts the money to your books.
Partial payments are fine. Record each one as it comes; the invoice tracks what's still owed until it's paid in full.
Set & forget

Recurring invoices

For customers you bill on a schedule (monthly retainers, memberships), set up a recurring invoice once and MTL creates each new invoice for you on time.

  1. Open Recurring from the sidebar.
  2. Set the customer, the line items, and how often it repeats.
  3. MTL generates each invoice on schedule — ready to send, or sent automatically, depending on your choice.
For your customer

The customer portal

Every invoice you send includes a link to a simple, branded page where your customer can view the invoice and pay online. There's nothing for them to install or sign up for.

Faster payments: an online pay link typically gets you paid sooner than "mail a check." Payments made through the portal flow back and mark the invoice paid.

Quick answers

Can I edit an invoice after sending it?

Yes — open it and edit, as long as its period isn't closed. If it's already paid, consider whether a change is appropriate before making it.

How do I bill a specific project?

Pick a Job on the invoice (see the Job Costing guide). The invoice then counts toward that job's profit.

The customer never got the email.

Check the customer's email address on their record, then re-send. You can also copy the invoice's payment link and send it yourself.
