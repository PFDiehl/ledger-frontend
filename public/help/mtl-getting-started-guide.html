Step 1

Set up your company

Your company details appear on your invoices and reports, so fill these in first.

Settings › Company

  1. Open Settings from the sidebar, then the Company tab.
  2. Enter your business name, address, email, and phone. Add a logo if you have one.
  3. Set your accounting method (cash or accrual) and your fiscal year start if they differ from the defaults.
  4. Save. That's the foundation — everything else builds on it.
Not sure cash vs. accrual? Most small businesses use cash basis — you count income when money arrives and expenses when you pay them. You can change it later, and your accountant can advise.
Step 2

Your chart of accounts

The chart of accounts is just the list of categories your money gets sorted into (Sales, Rent, Fuel, and so on). MTL can create a standard small-business chart for you in one click.

  1. Open Chart of accounts (under the Advanced section of the sidebar).
  2. If it's empty, choose to seed the standard chart — MTL adds a tidy, ready-to-use set of accounts.
  3. Rename or add accounts anytime to match how you think about your business. New ones are numbered for you automatically.
Why it matters: these categories are what you'll pick when you categorize bank transactions, bills, and expenses — and they're what your reports add up.
Step 3

Connect a bank

Connecting a bank pulls your transactions in automatically, so you're not typing them by hand.

  1. Open Banking and click 🔗 Connect a bank.
  2. A secure window (Plaid) opens — find your bank, log in with your bank's credentials, and pick the accounts to link. MTL never sees your bank password.
  3. Transactions start flowing into the For review tab, ready to categorize.
New to the bank feed? The Banking guide walks through categorizing, reconnecting, and importing statements by hand.
Step 4

Send your first invoice

  1. Open Invoices and click New invoice.
  2. Type a customer name (new ones are saved automatically), add your line items, and set the dates.
  3. Click Save draft to finish later, or Save & send to email it to the customer right away.
See the Invoicing & Getting Paid guide for recording payments and setting up recurring invoices.
Step 5

Invite a bookkeeper (optional)

If someone keeps your books for you, you can give them secure access to this company.

  • You have a bookkeeper: when they request access, you'll see a banner at the top of the app asking you to approve it. You choose Full (view & edit) or View only, and MTL keeps a signed record of the authorization.
  • You are the bookkeeper: see the For Bookkeepers guide for requesting access and switching between client companies.

Quick answers

Do I have to connect a bank?

No — you can import statements or enter transactions by hand. But connecting a bank saves the most time.

Can I run more than one business?

Yes. One login can hold several companies; switch between them from the company menu at the top.

What should I do first if I'm moving from QuickBooks?

See the Importing QuickBooks guide — you can pull your customers, vendors, invoices, bills, and expenses straight in.
