Getting access to a client's books

You don't need your client's password. MTL has a proper, consented access flow built for bookkeepers.

  1. Request access to the client's company (your firm sends the request).
  2. The client sees a banner in their own MTL asking them to approve it.
  3. They pick your access level and approve. You're in — their books appear in your company list.
Why this way: the client stays in control, grants access deliberately, and can revoke it at any time — which instantly ends your access.
What you can do

Access levels

LevelWhat it allows
Full (view & edit)The working bookkeeper level — do the actual books: categorize transactions, edit the chart of accounts, enter bills and invoices, run reports, connect QuickBooks, fill in company settings.
View onlySee everything and run reports, but make no changes. Good for a reviewer or a read-only stakeholder.
If you hit "you don't have permission to make changes": you're likely on View only. Ask the client to grant Full access so you can do the books.
Your paper trail

The authorization record

Every grant creates a dated authorization record — who approved it, the access level, and the consent text — which the client can download. It's clean documentation that you're working with permission, which protects both sides.

Many clients, one login

Switching between companies

Everything you're granted access to shows up under one login. Use the company menu at the top to switch from one client to the next — no logging out, no separate accounts.

The company switcher appears once you have access to two or more companies.

Quick answers

A client doesn't see my request.

It shows as a banner at the top of their app and in their access-approval screen. Have them log in and look for the "access request waiting for approval" prompt.

Can a client take access away?

Yes, anytime — and it ends your access immediately. That's by design; it keeps them in control of their own data.

I manage this client under my own brand.

That's the white-label / reseller side of MTL — ask MTL support about running clients under your firm's branding.
