Payroll in MountainTop Ledger

✓ Included in the  Startup plan · $15/mo

What payroll does today — and what it doesn't. MTL records payroll into your books. It doesn't run it.

In one line

MTL is your payroll bookkeeper, not your payroll service

You already know what you paid — gross pay, the taxes withheld, and the net check. MTL takes those numbers, posts a clean journal entry, and flows it into your P&L and reports. It does not calculate the withholding, cut the checks, or file the taxes — that's your payroll provider's job (or yours, by hand).

How it works

Record a run → it posts to the books

Enter a pay run on the Payroll page — by employee or as totals. When you save, MTL posts:

Dr Payroll & Wages (gross pay)4,000.00
Dr Payroll Taxes (employer side)306.00
Cr Payroll Clearing4,306.00
Then in Banking → categorize the payroll withdrawal from your bank to Payroll Clearing → the two cancel out, the cost lands once, and it reconciles.

Figures shown are an example. MTL also keeps year-to-date totals for each run.

The honest picture

What it does & what it doesn't

✓ What MTL payroll does

  • ✓Records each pay run — by employee or as totals
  • ✓Posts the journal entry to Payroll & Wages + Payroll Taxes automatically
  • ✓Flows payroll cost into your P&L and reports
  • ✓Reconciles cleanly via a Payroll Clearing account against your bank
  • ✓Keeps a pay-run history and year-to-date totals
  • ✓Cross-checks that net + withholding = gross and flags a mismatch

— What it doesn't do (yet)

  • ✕Calculate withholding — no federal/state income tax, Social Security or Medicare math. You supply the numbers.
  • ✕Print paychecks — no check printing.
  • ✕Pay anyone electronically — no direct deposit, no ACH, no sending a run to a payroll service.
  • ✕File or remit payroll taxes — no 941, 940, W-2s, or state filings.
  • ✕Produce pay stubs or an employee self-service portal.
  • ✕Track withholding as a liability through to remittance — the clearing account books the total cost, not separate tax liabilities.
Who it's for

A great fit for a small, in-house payroll

If you write a few payroll checks yourself and already handle the withholding — or your provider (Gusto, QuickBooks Payroll) pays the team and files the taxes — MTL's recorder does exactly what you need: it keeps payroll on the books correctly and in the reports, with nothing to reconcile by hand.

If you want MTL to calculate the taxes, cut the checks, and file the returns for you, that's full-service Managed Payroll — a separate service that's on the roadmap, not live today.

Are we “fully ready to go” with payroll?

Not for running payroll — yes for recording it

No, MTL is not set up to print payroll checks or send a pay run to a service to pay people electronically. Nothing in the program files taxes or moves money to employees.

Yes, MTL is ready to record payroll into the books and reports, today, on any plan. Think of it as the bookkeeping half of payroll — the other half (the calculating, paying, and filing) is done by a payroll provider until Managed Payroll is built.
