The two kinds of accounts

Every account in Banking is one of two types. Knowing which is which explains everything else in this guide.

TypeHow you spot itHow transactions arrive
Connected
(bank-linked)		 Shows a 🔗 Connected label and a Sync now option. Automatically. MTL pulls new transactions straight from the bank. You just categorize them.
Manual Shows Manual import and no 🔗 link. You bring them in — by importing a statement (PDF/CSV) or a Venmo/Cash App export. There's no login to sync.
Why it matters: a manual account can't "stop syncing" or "need reconnecting," because it was never syncing — it only ever holds what you import. Reconnecting and Sync are only for connected accounts.

Connecting a bank

  1. On Banking, click 🔗 Connect a bank.
  2. A secure window (Plaid) opens. Find your bank, log in with your bank's credentials, and pick the accounts to link. MTL never sees your bank password — that stays between you and your bank.
  3. The account appears with a 🔗 and starts pulling in transactions right away.
The daily rhythm

How a transaction becomes a book entry

New transactions don't post to your books on their own — you give each one a category first. That's the whole job, and it takes seconds per line.

  1. A new transaction arrives and lands in the For review tab, marked Uncategorized.
  2. You tell MTL what it is. On each line you can:
    • Add — pick a category and post it to the books.
    • Match — link it to an invoice or bill it pays off.
    • Split — divide one transaction across several categories.
  3. Once categorized, it moves to the Reviewed tab and is now in your books (a proper double-entry journal entry is created behind the scenes).
Tip: A big number in For review after a sync is normal — it's just the backlog that flowed in. Your Rules auto-categorize the familiar ones (see below), so you only hand-pick the rest.
"For review" vs "Reviewed": if you synced and thought nothing came in, check the For review tab — brand-new transactions always arrive there uncategorized, not in Reviewed. They're in; they just need a category.
Keeping it current

Syncing a connected bank

Connected accounts refresh on their own as your bank reports new activity. You can also pull the latest yourself anytime:

  1. Click the account card so it's selected.
  2. Click ⟳ Sync now (or Sync from bank at the top).
  3. MTL reports what it found — e.g. "Sync complete — 6 new, 0 updated." New items appear in For review.
"0 new" is usually fine — it just means there's been no new activity since the last sync. But if you know transactions are missing and keep getting "0 new," your connection most likely needs reconnecting — read on.
The important one

When a bank needs reconnecting

Every so often your bank requires you to log in again through its own security — after a password change, or just on the bank's periodic schedule. When that happens, syncing quietly stops until you re-authenticate.

MTL shows you when this happens: the account displays ⚠ Reconnect needed and a ↻ Reconnect button appears.

How to reconnect

  1. Select the account showing ⚠ Reconnect needed.
  2. Click ↻ Reconnect.
  3. The secure bank window opens — log in to your bank again (big banks may bounce you out to the bank's own site and back; that's expected).
  4. MTL confirms "Reconnected — N new transactions caught up," and everything the connection missed flows into For review.
Reconnect is not the same as Sync. If a connection has expired, clicking Sync now won't fix it — it just asks the bank for data using the old, expired login, which fails. Only Reconnect re-establishes the login and then pulls the backlog. Reach for Reconnect whenever you see the ⚠ badge.
Your history is safe. Reconnecting keeps the same account and all its existing transactions — it does not create a duplicate account or wipe anything. It simply refreshes the login.
By hand

Importing statements by hand

For manual accounts — or to fill a gap on any account — you can bring transactions in yourself. MTL reads several formats, and categorizing works exactly the same once they're in.

ButtonUse it for
✨ Import statement PDFA PDF statement from almost any bank or card. MTL's AI reads the pages and pulls out the transactions for you to confirm.
⬆ Import statementA CSV, .qbo, or .qfx file downloaded from your bank's website.
💚 Import Venmo / Cash AppA Venmo or Cash App activity export (CSV).
✎ Add manuallyTyping in a single transaction by hand.
  1. Click the import button that matches your file.
  2. Choose the file (or the account for a manual one). MTL shows a preview of the rows it found.
  3. Review the preview, fix anything that looks off, and confirm. The transactions drop into For review to be categorized — same as a bank sync.
Great for the gap after a lapse: if a connection was down for a while, you can either Reconnect (which catches up automatically) or import a statement PDF for the missing stretch — whichever is easier. MTL skips duplicates, so there's no harm if a few overlap.
A repair tool

The "Re-sync books" button

This one is easy to misunderstand, so here's exactly what it does — and doesn't do.

✓ What it doesTakes transactions you've already categorized and re-posts them into your double-entry books. It catches any that didn't make it in — for example, a transaction whose category was later renamed or removed — and posts them properly.
✕ What it does not doIt never contacts your bank. It does not re-download transactions, and it does not check your data against the bank. It only works with what's already in MTL.

When to use it: if you see a notice that "X categorized transactions are not in your books," or after you've renamed categories, click ↻ Re-sync books to post everything cleanly. It's safe to run anytime.

Renamed a category and transactions "fell off"? That's exactly what Re-sync books (and the Review & fix prompt) are for. Re-pick a current category for the flagged items, or run Re-sync books, and they post right back into the books.
Save yourself time

Rules & auto-categorizing

A Rule tells MTL "whenever a transaction's description contains this, categorize it as that." Once a rule exists, matching transactions are categorized automatically the moment they arrive — so a sync of a hundred transactions might leave only a handful for you to touch.

  1. Open ⚙ Rules on the Banking page.
  2. Add a rule: a word or phrase from the description (e.g. "Shell") and the category to apply (e.g. Fuel).
  3. From then on, every incoming "Shell" transaction lands already categorized in Reviewed.
Build rules as you go. Each time you categorize a recurring vendor by hand, consider adding a rule for it — your For review pile shrinks every month.

Quick answers

My bank stopped updating / transactions are missing.

Select the account. If you see ⚠ Reconnect needed, click ↻ Reconnect and log in to your bank again. If it says 🔗 Connected with a recent sync date, it's genuinely caught up — check the For review tab for new items.

I synced but nothing showed up.

Look in For review, not Reviewed — new transactions arrive uncategorized. If it truly pulled "0 new" and you're sure there's activity, the connection likely needs a Reconnect.

Why won't Venmo / my store card sync?

Those are usually manual accounts (no 🔗 link). They don't sync at all — bring their transactions in with Import statement or Import Venmo / Cash App.

I renamed some categories and transactions disappeared from my books.

They're flagged, not lost. Use the Review & fix prompt to re-pick a current category, or click ↻ Re-sync books.

Will reconnecting lose my history or duplicate the account?

No. Reconnect keeps the same account and all its transactions — it only refreshes the bank login.

Does "Re-sync books" pull new transactions from my bank?

No — that's Sync now. "Re-sync books" only re-posts already-categorized transactions into your ledger.
