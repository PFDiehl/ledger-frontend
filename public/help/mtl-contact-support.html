Help us help you

What to include in your message

A few details let us pinpoint the issue right away instead of trading emails back and forth:

  • Your company name in MTL (and which company, if you manage more than one).
  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead — including any error message, word for word.
  • A screenshot if you can grab one — it's often worth a dozen sentences.
  • Whether you were on the website or the phone app, and roughly when it happened.
