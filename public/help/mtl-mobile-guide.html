What you can do on your phone

The MTL app isn't a cut-down version — it handles the everyday money tasks, so work that used to wait for the office gets done on the spot.

You can…
Capture receiptsPhotograph a receipt and let MTL read the vendor, amount, and date — the standout feature for anyone in the field.
Log expensesRecord what you spent, attach the receipt image, and it's in the books.
Create & send invoicesBuild an invoice, set it recurring if needed, and send it from your phone.
Enter billsRecord a bill you owe while it's in front of you.
Add customers & vendorsCapture a new client or supplier the moment you meet them.
It's the same books. Everything you enter on the phone appears on the website instantly, and vice-versa — one set of records, two ways in.
One account

Signing in

  1. Open the MTL app and sign in with the same email and password you use on the website.
  2. Every company you own or have been given access to comes with you — no separate logins.
Bookkeepers: the companies your clients have granted you access to show up here too, just like on the web.
The standout

Capturing a receipt

This is the one your field crews will use every day: buy materials, photograph the receipt before you leave the counter, and it's handled.

  1. Go to Expenses and tap + Add Expense.
  2. Tap Scan Receipt and take (or choose) a photo.
  3. MTL reads the vendor, amount, and date and fills them in. Check them, pick a category, and the receipt image stays attached.
  4. Save. It's in the books, image and all — no shoebox of receipts at month-end.
Always glance at the scan. It's quick and accurate, but a crumpled or faint receipt can misread a number — a two-second check beats a correction later.
Job costing: to tag a field expense to a specific job, do it on the website afterward (Expenses or the job's + Assign) — the receipt you captured on the phone is already there waiting.
By hand

Logging an expense without a scan

You don't have to scan — you can type an expense in directly:

  1. Expenses → + Add Expense.
  2. Enter the vendor, amount, date, and a category. Add a receipt number or image if you have one.
  3. Save — it posts to your books right away.
Get paid faster

Invoices on the go

  1. Open Invoices and start a New Invoice.
  2. Pick (or add) the customer, set the date, and add your line items.
  3. Need it to repeat? Turn on recurring — MTL will prompt before each new one is created.
  4. Save and send. Your customer gets it with a pay link, same as from the website.
Finish a job, send the invoice before you leave. Billing on-site, while the work is fresh, is one of the fastest ways to get paid sooner.

Quick answers

Is it the same account as the website?

Yes — one login works everywhere, your companies follow you, and it's all one set of books. Something entered on the phone shows on the web right away.

The receipt scan read the wrong amount.

Just type over it before saving. The scan is a head start, not the final word.

Can I do my banking / reconcile on the phone?

The phone app is built for invoices, expenses, receipts, bills, and contacts. For bank categorizing and statement reconciliation, use the website (see the Banking guide).

Can my project managers use it without full access?

Give each person the access level that fits their role — talk to whoever administers your company about the right permissions.
