What the app is for

The MTL mobile app puts the day-to-day essentials in your pocket, so work that used to wait for a desk gets done on the spot.

The standout for trades and anyone on the move: receipt capture in the field. Buy materials, photograph the receipt, and it's in the books before you've left the parking lot.

One account

Signing in

  1. Open the MTL app and sign in with the same email and password you use on the web.
  2. All the companies you have access to come with you — no separate logins to manage.
One login covers every company you own or have been granted access to, on the phone just like on the web.
The main event

Capturing a receipt

  1. Tap to add an expense and photograph the receipt.
  2. MTL reads the vendor, amount, and date for you — check them and pick a category.
  3. For job costing, tag the expense to the right Job so the cost lands on that project.
  4. Save. It's in the books, receipt image attached, ready for whoever keeps them.
Why field crews love it: no more handing over a wad of faded receipts at month-end — costs are captured accurately, while the details are fresh, and tied to the right job.

Quick answers

Is it the same account as the website?

Yes — one login works everywhere, and your companies follow you.

Can my project managers use it without seeing everything?

Give them the access level that fits their role. Talk to whoever administers your company about the right permissions.

Do receipts I capture on the phone show up on the web?

Yes — it's all one set of books. An expense captured on the phone appears on the web immediately, receipt image and all.
