The two tabs

Where transactions live

  • For review — new, uncategorized transactions waiting for a category. Your to-do list.
  • Reviewed — transactions you've already categorized; posted in your books.
On each line

Three actions

  • Add — pick a category and post it to the books.
  • Match — link it to an invoice or bill it pays off.
  • Split — divide one transaction across several categories.
Month-end

Reconciling to a statement

Click ⚖ Reconcile, enter the statement ending date + balance, check off the transactions on your statement, and finish when the Difference reaches $0.00 (it turns green). The beginning balance pre-fills from your last reconciliation. Full steps →

Don't mix these up

The two rules to remember

Reconnect ≠ Sync. If an account shows "⚠ Reconnect needed," the Sync button won't help — only Reconnect re-establishes the bank login and then pulls the missing transactions.
Manual vs. connected. Accounts marked "Manual import" (no 🔗) never sync — feed them with Import statement / Import Venmo. Only 🔗 Connected accounts sync and reconnect.
