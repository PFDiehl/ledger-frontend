Reclassify Transactions

Move a whole category into another one — across every account, and across past years when you need to — with a preview before anything changes.

A guide for business owners & bookkeepers
The idea

When a category needs to move

Sometimes a category turns out to be the wrong home for a batch of transactions — everything filed under Research & Development really belongs in Software & Subscriptions, say. Fixing those one at a time is slow, and if they span several years it's easy to miss some.

Reclassify transactions does the whole move in one step: pick the category to move from, the category to move to, choose how far back to go, and MTL updates every matching transaction across all your accounts at once — after showing you exactly how many it will change.

Where to find it

On the Chart of Accounts page

Open Chart of Accounts. At the top right, next to “New account,” click Reclassify transactions. That opens the tool.

In MountainTop Ledger

How to reclassify, step by step

  1. Move from — choose the category you want to clear out (for example, Research & Development).
  2. Move to — choose where those transactions should go instead (for example, Software & Subscriptions).
  3. Which transactions — pick This year, All years, or a Custom range. It starts on This year.
  4. Preview — MTL tells you how many transactions will move: “This will change 142 transactions.” Nothing has changed yet.
  5. If that looks right, click Change 142 transactions. The move is applied across every account, and MTL confirms what it did.
Preview first, always. The count is a safe dry run — it looks but doesn't touch anything. You only commit the change on the second click, so you can check the number before you act.
What it protects

Locked work stays locked

Reclassify never moves a transaction that's already been reconciled or that falls in a closed accounting period — those are protected so a closed month can't be changed out from under you. When it skips any for that reason, it tells you: “3 reconciled and 5 in a closed period left unchanged.”

Choosing “All years”? Changing prior years can move numbers on reports and tax returns you've already filed. MTL shows a reminder when you pick it, so that choice is always a deliberate one.
Good to know

The quicker tool, for small fixes

For just a handful of transactions, there's a faster way: on the Banking page, tick the ones you want and change their category together — one account at a time. Use that for a quick cleanup, and use Reclassify when a category needs to move everywhere, especially across more than one year.

Tip: The “from” and “to” lists are your chart of accounts, grouped by type. If a category you expect isn't there, it may have been renamed — renaming an account already carries its history along with it, so there may be nothing left to move.
