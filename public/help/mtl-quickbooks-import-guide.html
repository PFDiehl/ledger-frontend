Before you start

Three things need to be true before an import will work. Checking them first saves a wasted trip through the sign-in screens.

You needWhy
QuickBooks Online (not Desktop)MTL connects to QuickBooks Online only. QuickBooks Desktop is a separate product it can't read.
Sign-in access to that QBO companyEither the client's own Intuit login, or your accountant login that's been invited to their company.
An Admin role in MTLThe Connect button is admin-only. If you see "Admin only" instead, ask the company owner to raise your role.
Not sure if the client is on Online or Desktop? If they log in through a web browser at qbo.intuit.com (no program installed), it's Online — you're good. If they open a QuickBooks program on their PC, that's Desktop — see the note at the end of this guide.
Step 1

Find the connection

The QuickBooks connection lives in Settings, under Integrations.

Settings › Integrations › QuickBooks Online

  1. Log in to MountainTop Ledger.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Settings.
  3. Open the Integrations tab.
  4. Find the QuickBooks Online card.
Step 2

Connect to QuickBooks

This is a one-time link between MTL and the client's QuickBooks company. MTL never sees the QuickBooks password — you sign in on Intuit's own page.

  1. Click Connect. You'll be sent to Intuit's sign-in page.
  2. Sign in with the Intuit account that owns — or has been invited to — the QuickBooks Online company you're importing.
  3. Authorize MTLedger App to access QuickBooks. You'll return to MTL and the card will read Connected.
Do NOT click "Sign in here instead." That link on Intuit's page is for QuickBooks Desktop — a different product MTL can't import from. Stay on the normal QuickBooks Online sign-in.
If Intuit offers to "start a free 30-day trial," stop. That means the account you signed in with has no existing QuickBooks Online company with data — it wants to create a brand-new, empty one. There'd be nothing to import, and the trial can turn into a paid subscription. Back out and sign in with the account that actually holds the client's books.
Step 3

Import the data

Once connected, an Import section appears on the same card. Click the buttons in this order — invoices and bills link to customers and vendors, so bring those in first.

  1. Import customers ← QuickBooks
  2. Import vendors ← QuickBooks
  3. Import invoices ← QuickBooks
  4. Import bills ← QuickBooks
  5. Import expenses ← QuickBooks

After each one, MTL shows a result line, for example:

Customers imported — 48 new · 3 matched & updated · 1 already linked · 0 failed
Safe to re-run. Clicking an import again does not create duplicates — existing records are matched and updated, not re-added. If a run shows failures, fix the flagged items and simply run it again.
Read the arrow

Which way is the data going?

The same card has two sets of buttons. The arrow tells you which direction the data flows — this is the one thing not to mix up.

ButtonDirectionWhat it does
… ← QuickBooksPulls INBrings QuickBooks data into MTL. This is what you want for a migration.
… → QuickBooks
(Sync section)		Pushes OUTSends your MTL data out to QuickBooks. Leave this alone when you're importing.
Finish up

After importing

  1. Spot-check the Customers, Vendors, Invoices, Bills, and Expenses pages so the counts look right.
  2. Connect the client's bank and card accounts under Banking for ongoing transactions going forward.
  3. Pull in historical bank transactions through the bank connection, or by importing a .qbo / .qfx / .ofx file on the Banking page.
What the import covers: customers, vendors, invoices, bills, and expenses. It does not move bank transactions — those come in through Banking (see the Banking Guide).

Troubleshooting

The Connect button is greyed out or says "coming soon."

The QuickBooks connection isn't enabled for this environment yet. Contact MountainTop Ledger support.

It says "Admin only" instead of Connect.

You need an Admin role on this MTL company. Ask the company owner to raise your role, then reload Settings.

Intuit wants to start a free 30-day trial.

You're signed into an Intuit account with no existing QuickBooks Online company. Back out and sign in with the account that actually has the client's books — don't start the empty trial.

Some records show "failed."

Open the error list shown under the result line, fix the flagged items in QuickBooks if needed, and re-run the import — it's safe to run again.

The client is on QuickBooks Desktop, not Online.

MTL imports from QuickBooks Online only. For a Desktop client, export their lists and transactions from Desktop and bring them in via MTL's CSV / statement imports, or have them move to QuickBooks Online first. Ask MTL support for the current Desktop path.

Did I just duplicate everything by clicking twice?

No. Imports match on existing records and update them rather than adding copies, so re-running is harmless.
