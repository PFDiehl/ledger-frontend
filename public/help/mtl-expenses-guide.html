Start here

Add an expense

  1. Open Expenses → New expense.
  2. Enter the vendor, amount, and date.
  3. Pick a category (from your chart of accounts), a payment method, and — for job costing — a Job.
  4. Save. The expense posts to your books right away.
Expense vs. bill: use an expense for something you've already paid. Use a bill for something you owe and will pay later (see the Bills, Vendors & 1099s guide).
The time-saver

Scan a receipt

Instead of typing, let MTL read the receipt for you.

  1. On the New Expense form, click 📷 Scan receipt to auto-fill.
  2. Choose a photo of the receipt (JPG or PNG).
  3. MTL reads the vendor, amount, and date and fills them in. Check them, pick a category, and save.
Always double-check the scan. It's quick and accurate, but a crumpled or faint receipt can misread a number — a two-second glance saves a correction later.
Keep the proof

Attach a receipt image

You can keep the receipt image attached to the expense for your records — handy at tax time or if you're ever audited.

  1. Open an existing expense.
  2. In the Receipt section, Upload Receipt (or Replace / Remove an existing one).
  3. Click the image anytime to view it full size.
Sorting it right

Categories & jobs

The category comes from your chart of accounts — it's how the expense lands in the right spot on your Profit & Loss. If your chart isn't set up yet, MTL offers a sensible default list.

If you use job costing, pick a Job so the expense counts as a cost on that project.

Out on the job

Capturing in the field

The MTL mobile app is built for exactly this moment: your project manager buys materials and snaps the receipt before leaving the counter.

The expense (and its photo) goes straight into the books — and can be tagged to the right job — so costs stay current and nobody's reconciling a shoebox of receipts at month-end.

See the The Mobile App guide for signing in and capturing receipts on your phone.
At a glance

Quick answers

The scan read the wrong amount.

Just type over it before saving. The scan is a head start, not the final word.

Can I attach a PDF receipt?

Receipt images (JPG/PNG) attach to an expense. For a bank/card charge, you can also match it to the expense in Banking.

Where do these show up in reports?

Under the category you chose, on your Profit & Loss — and, if tagged, on that job's profit.
