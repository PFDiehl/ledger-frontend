This Privacy Policy explains how MountainTop Ledger, LLC (“MountainTop Ledger,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our web application, mobile application, and related services (together, the “Service”). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

MountainTop Ledger provides bookkeeping and accounting software for small businesses. You can reach us about privacy at contact@mountaintopledger.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information — your name, email address, password, business name, and (optionally) phone number when you create an account.
  • Business and financial records — the data you enter or import to run your books, such as invoices, expenses, bills, customers, vendors, and reports.
  • Receipt and document images — photos or files you upload (for example, receipts captured with your device camera or photo library).
  • Payment information — when you subscribe to a paid plan, your payment is processed by our payment provider. We do not store your full card number; we retain limited billing details such as plan, status, and the last four digits of your card.
  • Communications — information you provide when you contact support or correspond with us.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data — such as app interactions, log data, device type, operating system, and general diagnostic information used to operate and improve the Service.

Information from connected services

  • Bank connections — if you choose to connect a financial account, we (through a secure aggregation provider) receive transaction and balance information from that account to help you reconcile your books. You control which accounts you connect and can disconnect them at any time.
  • Sign-in providers — if you sign in using a third-party provider such as Google, we receive basic profile information (like your name and email) to create or access your account.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • provide, operate, and maintain the Service and your account;
  • process invoices, expenses, reports, bank reconciliation, and other bookkeeping features you use;
  • process subscription payments and manage billing;
  • respond to your requests and provide customer support;
  • secure the Service, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our terms;
  • improve and develop features; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your business or financial records for advertising.

4. How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Service providers — trusted companies that process data on our behalf, such as cloud hosting and database providers, our payment processor, our receipt-image storage provider, and (if you connect a bank) our account-aggregation provider. They may only use the information to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety — when required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of MountainTop Ledger, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. After you close your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable period, unless we are required to retain it for legal, tax, accounting, or security reasons.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction — you can view and update most account and business information directly in the app.
  • Deletion — you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us at contact@mountaintopledger.com.
  • Bank connections — you can disconnect any linked financial account at any time.
  • Marketing — you can opt out of non-essential emails using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws. Contact us to exercise them and we will respond as required by law.

8. Children’s privacy

The Service is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

We operate in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, you understand that your information may be processed and stored in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your location.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated Policy.
