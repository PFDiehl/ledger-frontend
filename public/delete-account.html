Delete your account and data

You can request deletion of your MountainTop Ledger account and the personal data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted, and how long it takes.

How to request deletion

Email us at contact@mountaintopledger.com from the email address on your account, with the subject line “Delete my account.”

So we can verify the request, please send it from the email address you use to sign in. If you have trouble emailing from that address, contact us and we will confirm your identity another way before deleting anything.

What gets deleted

When your request is verified, we delete your account and the personal data tied to it, including:

  • your account profile (name, email, phone, password);
  • your business and financial records created in the app — invoices, expenses, bills, customers, vendors, and reports;
  • receipt images and documents you uploaded;
  • and any other personal data associated with your account.

What we may keep

We may retain a limited amount of information where we are required to by law — for example, records needed for tax, accounting, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. We keep such records only for as long as the law requires and then delete them. We do not use retained data for any other purpose.

How long it takes

We process account-deletion requests within 30 days of verifying your request, and usually sooner. We will confirm by email once your account and data have been deleted.

Questions

For anything related to deleting your account or data, contact us at contact@mountaintopledger.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
